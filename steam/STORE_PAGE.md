## Short description (max 300 characters)

NetHack 3.7, the classic roguelike, in first person. Walk the Mazes of Menace in a voxel world where NetHack still decides every rule and every roll of the dice: every monster, item, trap and prayer is NetHack's own.

## About this game

**VoxHack is NetHack 3.7 in first person.** Walk the Mazes of Menace in a 3D voxel world, where NetHack still decides every rule and every roll of the dice.

**The real NetHack underneath.** The whole of NetHack 3.7 runs the game: every monster, item, trap, prayer and death is NetHack's own, from the Gnomish Mines to the Astral Plane.

**A dungeon you walk through.** Move freely, look around, swing at what's in front of you. Time crawls while you stand still and runs as you move.

**Built from NetHack's levels.** Every level is built in 3D from NetHack's own map, with special levels made by hand as places of their own.

**Mouse and keyboard play.** Aim with the crosshair, act with a right-click wheel, and keep NetHack's command line one key away.

**Sound you can place.** Monsters, doors and boulders sound from where they are, muffled behind walls; every place has its own echo; shopkeepers, priests and your god speak their lines with SAM, the 1982 speech synthesizer.

**NetHackWiki built in.** Point at any monster, item or trap and press F2: its NetHackWiki page opens right in the game. Only what you know: an unidentified potion opens the general Potion page.

**Open source.** VoxHack is free software under the NetHack General Public License; the full source is on GitHub: https://github.com/MrEveryDay98/VoxHack

*VoxHack is based on NetHack by the NetHack DevTeam and runs on the Luanti engine. It is not an official NetHack release.*

## Early Access questions

**Why Early Access?**
NetHack is enormous, and every part of it needs to work in first person: hundreds of monsters, items and special levels, each built in 3D. Players finding what feels wrong (a level that doesn't fit, an action that's awkward from behind a crosshair) shapes it more than anything I could test alone.

**Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?**
About a year (estimate).

**How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?**
Every special level built by hand, more monster models and animations, controller support, more sound and voice, polish everywhere, and whatever players ask for most.

**What is the current state of the Early Access version?**
NetHack 3.7 runs the whole game, so every role, race, monster and item is there, from the first level to the Astral Plane. Many special levels are hand-built; the rest are built from NetHack's own maps. Not every part has been played through in 3D yet: expect rough edges.

**How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?**
Bug reports and ideas through the Steam discussions and GitHub; updates often, with notes on what changed. The source is open, so anyone can look at how it works.
